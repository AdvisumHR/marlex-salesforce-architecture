What this programme is

Marlex's recruitment system runs today on TargetRecruit, a product it licenses. The programme replaces it with a solution Marlex builds and owns itself, on the same Salesforce platform. Marlex stops renting the engine and owns it — no vendor lock-in, and its own roadmap. The aim is to match what the business does today and improve how people actually use it, not to copy the old tool screen-for-screen.

The rebuild happens inside the same Salesforce org that runs TargetRecruit today — not a new, separate system. The new and the old run side by side, and each part of recruitment switches over to the new build once it is proven, retiring the matching TargetRecruit piece. So the systems that already feed data into Salesforce keep working unchanged, and switching over means pointing a process at the new build rather than moving to a different system.

The recruitment rebuild is the priority; almost everything else follows it. Two tracks run in parallel with it and do not wait: the integration layer (MuleSoft), built one system at a time, and Marketing Cloud, which runs finite campaigns now and re-points onto the new model later. Three things come after the rebuild is live: the sharing-and-access model, Axxon's onboarding onto the standard, and the rollout to other Marlex countries.

Once the rebuilt platform is proven and Marlex is running on it, that same build is reused — Axxon adopts it on its own org, and Portugal, Italy and the Philippines roll out from it — so each new brand or country starts from a proven base rather than a fresh build.

See the programme roadmap for the full sequence and the gates between phases.

The delivery projects

Each project has its own readable roadmap — what it delivers, in what order, and why. A shared foundation epic appears in more than one project, so the per-project epic counts intentionally overlap and do not sum to the total above.

ATS-RB
The recruitment-platform rebuild
The core rebuild — Marlex's recruitment platform is re-created as its own native Salesforce build, in the same org as the rented product, one component at a time until the rented product can be switched off. The scope is what is coupled to that product plus the recruitment features still needed; the requirements gate draws the line.
15 epics50 deliverables
SHARE
The sharing & access model
Bringing the internal HR team onto the recruitment platform and making the access model private where it must be — the two are one initiative, because HR's confidential work cannot sit in an open model even briefly. More than a sharing change: it builds what HR needs on the new platform and refactors who can see which records, whether the boundary is a country or an internal team.
3 epics11 deliverables
AX-STD
Axxon onboarding (CRM, recruitment, back office and NET4)
Axxon onboards onto the group standard by reuse-by-default — replicate the Marlex build and deploy the group-shared capabilities, building Axxon-specific only where a real business need or French regulation demands it — on its own separate Salesforce org, across CRM, recruitment, back office and the NET4 finance connection.
8 epics50 deliverables
MULE
The integration layer (MuleSoft)
The integration layer — cross-cutting foundations, then the per-system lanes consolidated into one track, built one lane at a time so no single integration blocks the others; systems that only need a secure direct link connect straight to Salesforce.
4 epics23 deliverables
PORTAL
The candidate & recruiter portals
The candidate and recruiter portals — the web front-ends that live today on the platform the programme is retiring. The Salesforce programme owns the connection to them, not the portals themselves; the build sits with an external partner, and its scope, owner and — above all — its timing against the platform switchover are still open.
open track
EXP
Group expansion (Portugal, Italy, the Philippines)
Marlex group expansion — reuse the proven Spain platform: Portugal and Italy switched on inside the shared org via one reusable playbook, the Philippines stood up on its own brand-org, all after Spain is live and the standard is classified.
3 epics15 deliverables
MKT
Marketing Cloud
Marketing Cloud in two waves, in one org throughout — finite campaigns now against the current recruitment data to earn back the licence, then a re-point onto the rebuilt model after go-live.
2 epics5 deliverables
EVOL
Evolution — new requirements after go-live
New requirements captured after the rebuild cutover. The org is already live by then, so this is ongoing evolution rather than a further go-live — sized as each need arrives.
future backlog
